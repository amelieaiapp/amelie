AMELIE·AI Planner: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 June 2026

This policy explains, in plain English, what AMELIE·AI Planner ("the app", "AMELIE", "we") does and does not do with your information. The short version: the app runs on your own device, AMELIE has no servers, and we do not collect anything about you. A few features connect outward, and those are explained honestly below: the optional AI features, and the links to outside supplier websites (some of which are affiliate links or paid sponsored listings).

The summary

  • AMELIE works offline. Your projects, tasks, costs and notes are stored only on your own device.
  • AMELIE has no servers. We do not collect, receive, see or store your project data or personal information, and we have nothing to sell or share.
  • The optional AI features, if you choose to use them, send a project summary directly from your device to Anthropic (the AI provider), using your own Anthropic account.
  • The supplier directory contains links to outside websites. Some are affiliate links, and some listings are paid sponsorships. Neither gives any business access to your data.

1. Data stored on your device

Everything you create in AMELIE, including projects, schedules, tasks, costs, registers, site diary entries and settings, is saved locally on the device where you use the app, in your browser or app storage. It stays on your device. It is not uploaded to us, because we have no servers to upload it to. You can export your own data as a backup file at any time, and you can delete it from within the app.

2. We do not collect personal information

AMELIE contains no analytics, tracking or telemetry of your usage. We do not collect your name, email, location, usage statistics or any other personal information. There is no account system and no database of users. Because we hold no user data, there is nothing for us to sell, share or pass to advertisers or sponsors, and we never do.

3. The optional AI features

AMELIE includes optional AI features (the AI Review and the AI Conflict Check fix-suggestions). These never run on their own. They run only when you explicitly choose to use them, and only after you have set up your own AI access.

When you run an AI feature:

  • A summary of the relevant project data is sent directly from your device to Anthropic, the company that provides the Claude AI service, at their API (api.anthropic.com).
  • This uses your own Anthropic API key, which you create with Anthropic and which is stored only on your device (or not stored at all, if you choose "ask me each time"). AMELIE has no servers and never receives, sees or stores your data or your key. It goes straight from your device to Anthropic.
  • What is sent: task names, dates, statuses, costs, material totals and hire dates. What is not sent: your name, contact details, supplier details or your notes.
  • You can set a spending cap on your Anthropic account, and you can delete or revoke your key in the Anthropic console at any time.
  • According to Anthropic, data sent to their API is not retained and is not used to train their models. Anthropic's handling of that data is governed by Anthropic's own privacy policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

If you never use the AI features, no project data ever leaves your device.

4. Supplier directory and links to other websites

AMELIE includes a supplier directory and links to outside trade websites and map searches (for example builders merchants, timber merchants, tool hire and online trade suppliers). When you click one of these, you leave AMELIE and go to a website run by someone else. Those websites have their own privacy policies and their own handling of your information, which we do not control and are not responsible for. We do not pass any of your data to them; you simply arrive at their site as any visitor would.

5. Affiliate links

Some links to suppliers are affiliate links. If you buy something after following one, AMELIE·AI may earn a small commission. This is at no extra cost to you: the price you pay is the same, and it does not affect which suppliers are shown to you. We do not receive any personal or payment information about your purchase, only, where applicable, an anonymous confirmation from the retailer's affiliate scheme that a referral occurred.

6. Sponsored listings and advertising

Some supplier listings may be sponsored, meaning a business has paid to appear in the directory. Sponsorship buys placement only. It does not give any business access to your data, your projects or your identity, and we do not share or sell user data to sponsors or advertisers, because we hold none. Sponsorship enquiries from businesses are handled separately and never involve your information.

7. Purchases

If you buy the one-time Pro upgrade, that purchase is handled entirely by the app store (for example the Microsoft Store) under the store's own terms and privacy policy. AMELIE does not process or receive your payment details.

8. Children

AMELIE is a professional construction planning tool intended for adult and business use. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
